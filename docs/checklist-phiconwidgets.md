# Physical checklist: Phicon Widgets

Run this before switching Phicon Widgets to `available` in `src/data/tools.json`, and after any
change to `src/tools/phiconwidgets/core/`. The block net is PaperPhicons'; do the
[PaperPhicons checklist](checklist-paperphicons.md) first, or at least its first block.

App: `/apps/phiconwidgets/`. Print at **Actual size**; cut the `_calib_` file first.

## Blocks

### 1. The app's example: 30 mm cube, marker 0 on the side wall, one 20 mm circle

- [ ] No notes in the app
- [ ] The hole is cut clean through, centred on the base face (±0.3 mm), 20.0 mm across (±0.2)
- [ ] The block folds closed with the hole on top; the marker is centred on the side wall

### 2. Slot and button: 60 × 40 × 20, marker 90 size 12 with offset −12, horizontal pill 40 × 10 at (0, 10), square 8 × 6 at (20, −12)

- [ ] The marker sits above the slot, clear of it and of the square
- [ ] The pill measures 40 × 10 mm with round ends; the square 8 × 6 mm
- [ ] Nothing is cut into the fold lines

### 3. Two copies: 40 × 50 × 15, marker 55 size 10 on the side with offset 5; vertical pill 30 × 12 at (−8, 0), square 10 × 20 at (10, 5), circle Ø6 at (10, −17)

- [ ] Both blocks and all their holes are cut where they are printed (the sketch's cut file got
      the second block wrong)

### 4. Warnings

- [ ] Moving a cut-out onto the marker shows the "cuts into the marker" note
- [ ] Moving it past the edge of the face shows the "reaches past the face" note

## With a widget

- [ ] A knob, slider or button fits through its hole and moves freely (note the play you want:
      ______ mm)
- [ ] The camera still detects the marker with the widget in place
