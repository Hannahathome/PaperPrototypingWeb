## About

The [web app](../../apps/paperpolyhedra/) is a basic version of PaperPolyhedra. It makes
nets for **prisms and frustums** with any number of sides: a strip of panels with tabs, a glue
flap and hook tab to close it, and two lids. You can add a **fill colour** and **images**
(one across the strip, one per panel, and on the lids). It exports the usual three files:
the PDF to print and the two SVG files for the cutter.

Several shapes can share one sheet: add copies, or **import a JSON file** of shapes (for
example from [DataPhysicalisation](../dataphysicalisation/)), then drag them into place on the
preview or let the app arrange them. It warns when shapes overlap, leave the cutting area or
reach the corners where the calibration crosses are cut.

Its nets are identical to the Processing version's (checked to within 0.001 mm). For
everything else (per-edge shapes, Kresling patterns, markers, connected shapes, base plates,
JSON import) use the [Processing version](https://github.com/Hannahathome/PaperPrototyping/tree/main/PaperPolyhedra).

The web app has been checked with real prints and cuts (September 2026).
