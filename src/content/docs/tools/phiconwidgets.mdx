## About

The [web app](../../apps/phiconwidgets/) makes [PaperPhicons](../paperphicons/) blocks with holes
cut into their base face: squares and rectangles, pills (rounded slots, across or along the
face) and circles. Each hole has a size and a position from the centre of the face, and every
block on the sheet gets the same holes. Use them to let a knob, a slider or a button reach
through the block, while the ArUco marker (on the base, moved aside with its offset, or on the
side wall) lets a camera track it.

The app warns when a hole reaches past the face into the folds, or cuts into the marker or its
white margin, which would stop a camera from detecting it.

It is based on the TEI'27 sketch `PaperPhicons_Boilerplate_v1_1_Cutout`, which is not on
GitHub, so there is no link to it. The nets and holes match that sketch's exports; like the
PaperPhicons web app, it follows the shared print-and-cut layout and fixes the sketch's cut-file
spacing when there are several blocks.

The web app is in beta until printed blocks have been checked.
