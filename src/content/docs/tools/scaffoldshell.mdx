## About

The [web app](../../apps/scaffoldshell/) is a basic version of ScaffoldShell: everything the
[PaperPolyhedra](../paperpolyhedra/) web app does, plus a **scaffold** for each shape, a
3D-printable strut frame that goes inside the folded shell. The frame is sized from the shell
itself (its radii come from the shell's perimeters, minus a clearance you set) and turned so
its wall struts sit in the shell's folded corners.

Add **rigs** to hold electronics (M5Atom, M5Core and M5Core+Ext presets, or any size), and let
a rig cut a **window** in the paper where its face meets the shell, so a screen or button shows
through. The download contains the usual print and cut files plus one **OpenSCAD** file per
scaffold, identical to what the [Processing version](https://github.com/Hannahathome/PaperPrototyping/tree/main/ScaffoldShell)
writes.

**Set the clearance from your own measurements** before printing: fold a shell, measure it,
and enter the difference. For frustums, check the note in the app about the frame height.

The web app is in beta until a printed scaffold has been checked inside a folded shell.
